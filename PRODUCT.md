# BMYGYM

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-specified Next.js, React, strict TypeScript, App Router, Node.js runtime and Tailwind CSS. GitHub to Vercel. No separate server, database or authentication in phase one.

## Users

Private clients, architects, designers, yacht professionals, hospitality groups, developers and private aviation specialists evaluating a bespoke longevity environment.

## Product Purpose

Explain the independent, founder-led design house and convert interest into a private project enquiry. The first paid engagement is the BMYGYM Private Longevity Blueprint: concept and strategy before implementation.

## Positioning

Work begins with the individual, their architecture and lifestyle. Selected existing technologies, specialist collaboration and original bespoke concepts are possible. This is not an equipment shop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

One English marketing landing page with anchors home, philosophy, environments, approach, vision and enquiry. Land, sea and air receive equal presence. The user additionally confirmed a configurable email integration in this phase. Enquiries go to hello@bmygym.com. No publication until final approval. No invented testimonials, prices, certifications or completed-project claims.

## Brand Commitments

Quiet luxury, warm ivory, cream, sand, muted taupe and restrained champagne accents. Editorial serif paired with minimal sans-serif. Generous space, selective architectural imagery and subtle accessible motion. Preserve the supplied English positioning and founder Bojana Pesic.

## Evidence on Hand

Three supplied briefs and thirteen images (including one duplicate). Images are concept visualisations. A generated AERO ONE image was subsequently created at the user's request: an ivory private-jet fitness studio with a prominent Pilates reformer, integrated cable training, arranged dumbbells and oval windows above the clouds, visibly captioned “BMYGYM AERO ONE · Concept visualisation.” It does not represent a real or certified installation. No production logo artwork, business registration details, approved legal texts, email provider credentials, GitHub remote or Vercel project supplied.

## Product Principles

Begin with the client. Make the paid concept phase understandable. Present concepts honestly. Let architecture lead imagery. Minimise browser JavaScript and dependencies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled forms, image alternatives and reduced motion. Verify widths 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels.

## Open Decisions

Legal documents require the actual legal entity and approval. AERO ONE remains a concept subject to aircraft-specific technical feasibility, safety and certification requirements. Email sender/domain verification and deployment credentials remain configuration tasks.
