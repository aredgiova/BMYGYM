# BMYGYM

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-specified Next.js, React, strict TypeScript, App Router, Node.js runtime and Tailwind CSS. GitHub to Vercel. No separate server, database or authentication in phase one.

## Users

Two client profiles share one site: Private Clients who own villas, residences, superyachts or private aircraft; and Professional Partners including property developers, architects, interior designers, yacht designers and luxury hospitality operators. Projects range from a single private environment to larger property and hospitality developments.

## Product Purpose

Explain BMYGYM's role as an independent, founder-led creator and integrator and convert interest into a project enquiry. The first professional phase is the BMYGYM Private Longevity Blueprint: conceptualisation and design under a formal, paid commission, with scope, deliverables and fees agreed before work starts. Subsequent design, product selection, manufacturing coordination and integration phases may be commissioned separately according to the project's needs.

## Positioning

“BMYGYM creates and integrates bespoke longevity environments for exceptional properties across LAND, SEA & AIR.” This proposition appears in the opening section. BMYGYM conceives, designs, selects and coordinates complete environments with architects, interior designers, specialist manufacturers and other expert partners. Its own Signature Concepts are combined with carefully selected premium products from other manufacturers. It does not need to manufacture every integrated product. Work begins with the individual, their architecture and lifestyle.

## Capabilities and Constraints

One marketing landing page in five languages: English is primary at `/`, with German at `/de`, French at `/fr`, Spanish at `/es` and Russian at `/ru`. Navigation, page copy, metadata, image alternatives, enquiry fields and messages, and legal information are localised. Language is selected explicitly using a discreet header control; it does not require a cookie or automatic browser-language redirect. Anchors remain home, philosophy, environments, approach, vision and enquiry. Land, sea and air receive equal presence.

The enquiry form distinguishes private clients from professional partners and supports residence, yacht, luxury hospitality, private aviation and other bespoke projects. The selected locale accompanies the enquiry. A configurable email integration sends enquiries to hello@bmygym.com; confirmed provider delivery must not be simulated. No invented testimonials, prices, certifications or completed-project claims. Publication follows the user's deployment authorization. This documentation records repository changes, not a deployment.

## Brand Commitments

Quiet luxury, warm ivory, cream, sand, muted taupe and restrained champagne accents. Editorial serif paired with minimal sans-serif. Generous space, selective architectural imagery and subtle accessible motion. Preserve the supplied English positioning, founder Bojana Pesic and brand names including Private Longevity Blueprint, Signature Concepts, BMYGYM Fitness Wall, BMYGYM Recovery Capsule and AERO ONE. Provide considered editorial equivalents for descriptive copy and local explanations of brand terms; see `docs/i18n.md`.

## Product Principles

Begin with the client. Make the paid concept phase understandable. Present concepts honestly. Let architecture lead imagery. Minimise browser JavaScript and dependencies.

Responsive navigation changes to the desktop arrangement at 1440px; at widths of 600px or less the separate header enquiry button is hidden while enquiry remains available in the menu and hero.
