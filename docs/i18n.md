# BMYGYM language and editorial policy

Updated 28 October 2026. This document records the five-language repository implementation and copy decisions. It does not assert deployment, completion of runtime verification, native-human translation review or legal approval.

## Languages and routes

English remains the unprefixed primary route. `src/proxy.ts` rewrites unprefixed pages internally to the English route. The `/en` internal route also remains accessible, with canonical and language links pointing to the unprefixed equivalent. It must not redirect back to `/`, because that would loop when the rewrite is resolved. Visitors select their language explicitly; there is no automatic language redirect, language cookie or local-storage requirement for language selection.

Legal slugs remain `legal`, `privacy`, `cookies` and `accessibility`; their titles and contents are localised. Examples are `/privacy`, `/de/privacy`, `/fr/privacy`, `/es/privacy` and `/ru/privacy`. The same pattern applies to the other legal pages. The four documents supplied on 28 October 2026 replace the drafts and show their localised update date. They identify Bojana Pesic and the supplied Valencia business address. The privacy and cookie policies also describe the implemented enquiry and storage behaviour. Legal pages remain excluded from indexing. Translation does not supply missing business facts or approve legal texts.

The root document sets the correct `lang` attribute. Localised home and legal metadata specify canonical URLs, language alternatives and English `x-default`. The sitemap includes the five home variants only when site indexing is enabled. Existing home anchors keep their stable IDs across languages.

## Content organisation

`src/i18n/config.ts` defines locales, their native names and URL helpers. Each language has a `*-home.json` and a `*-common.json` dictionary. Home dictionaries hold landing-page content, captions and image alternatives. Common dictionaries hold navigation, footer, enquiry form labels and placeholders, validation, API responses, legal pages, accessibility copy and not-found messages. `dictionaries.ts` loads the selected pair; `types.ts` describes their shared structure.

All five dictionaries must retain the English keys, value types, array lengths and element ordering. Complete locale dictionaries are supplied rather than filling missing text with English. Brand names and genuinely shared words may match the English source. Preserve message constraints such as character limits in every language.

The enquiry form includes client profile, project type, optional company and optional location. Private and professional profiles share the same flow. Translated option labels map to stable internal values, and the selected locale accompanies the request. Client and server errors, sending state, success state and email fallback all use the chosen language. A successful message must follow the configured provider's response; translated success text is not evidence that email delivery is configured or tested.

`home.workWithUs` contains the localised title and two editorial blocks for private clients and professional partners. Their links use `?client=private#enquiry` or `?client=professional#enquiry`, retaining the current language path and other parameters. The mounted form responds without discarding entered text. A deliberate profile link overrides the manual profile choice; ordinary section navigation preserves it. Browser history and initial URL selection are supported, and language links retain the selected URL profile. Unknown profile values leave the selector empty.

## Commercial meaning to preserve

The opening section must make the creator-and-integrator role clear: BMYGYM conceives, designs, selects and coordinates complete environments, combining its own Signature Concepts with carefully selected premium products from specialist manufacturers. Private clients and professional partners are both addressed, including larger property and hospitality projects.

The Private Longevity Blueprint starts through a formal, paid commission. Scope, deliverables and fees are agreed before design begins. Later design, selection, manufacturing coordination and integration can be commissioned according to project needs. Do not imply free design work, include invented fees or make every later phase part of the initial commission.

AERO ONE is a transformable recovery and longevity chair concept for private aviation, in development, neither certified nor commercially available. Aircraft-specific technical feasibility, safety and aeronautical certification requirements apply to its development and integration. Its new supplied image and its localised caption must not suggest an installed or certified product. Other Signature Concepts remain design visions in development.

The requested complete BMYGYM Environment visualisation and immersive or virtual tour belong to a later phase. No new image or tour is introduced by this localisation update.

## Selector, typography and mobile behaviour

The header selector shows a compact uppercase language code, such as `EN`, with a restrained chevron. Its disclosure lists all five native language names and codes, with the current language identified. Links switch to the corresponding page and preserve the query and anchor, including when opened in another tab. Escape closes the disclosure and restores focus; an outside pointer also closes it. Both selector labels and navigation labels are localised.

The desktop header navigation begins at 1440px to accommodate longer translated labels. Below that width, navigation uses the disclosure menu. At 600px or less, the separate header enquiry button is hidden; enquiry remains available from the mobile menu and hero. Content wraps without shrinking supporting text below the established readable size.

Bodoni Moda and Manrope continue to serve Latin text locally. Russian sans-serif text uses the local Manrope Cyrillic subset. Because Bodoni Moda lacks Cyrillic coverage, local Cormorant Garamond normal and italic faces provide the Cyrillic serif fallback. All supplemental fonts originate from official Google Fonts sources and retain their SIL Open Font License notices, including `public/fonts/Cormorant-Garamond-OFL.txt`. See `docs/asset-manifest.md` for files and sizes.

## Informational cookie notice

The notice is localised in all five `*-common.json` dictionaries through `cookieNotice.title`, `body`, `policy` and `dismiss`; `footer.cookieSettings` labels the reopening control. The Spanish dismissal is “Entendido”. The policy link resolves to the current language's `/cookies` page. Keep the notice and cookie policy aligned: no analytics or advertising, a 180-day dismissal preference, and no consent to optional tracking implied by closing the notice.

One `localStorage` key, `bmygym.cookie-notice.v1`, is shared across languages on the same origin. Language changes must retain a valid dismissal rather than create a separate preference. The URL still determines the language. See [cookie-notice.md](cookie-notice.md) for the UX and storage contract and [validation.md](validation.md) for the 28 October local checks across five languages and four widths.
