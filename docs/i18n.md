These are enquiry and appointment-request pages: no published dates, destinations, prices, availability or confirmed reservations are invented. Localised home, legal and booking-page metadata specify canonical URLs, language alternatives and English `x-default`. When site indexing is enabled, the sitemap includes all seven home variants and both additional pages in all seven languages. Preview indexing remains disabled. Existing home anchors keep their stable IDs across languages.

## Content organisation

`src/i18n/config.ts` defines locales, their native names and URL helpers. Each language has `*-home.json`, `*-common.json`, `*-booking.json` and `*-retreats.json` dictionaries. Home dictionaries hold landing-page content, captions and image alternatives. Common dictionaries hold navigation, footer, enquiry form labels and placeholders, validation, API responses, legal pages, accessibility copy and not-found messages. `dictionaries.ts` loads the selected home/common pair; `types.ts` describes their shared structure. `booking.ts` loads booking copy using the `retreat` and `private-consultation` intent keys. Booking dictionaries provide page introductions, availability explanations, form headings, message fields, submit buttons and request-specific confirmation messages; common contact, timeline, privacy and validation copy is reused. `retreats.ts` loads the dedicated Retreats editorial content, imagery captions and future yacht experience copy.

All seven language versions in each dictionary category must retain the English keys, value types, array lengths and element ordering. Complete locale dictionaries are supplied rather than filling missing text with English. Brand names and genuinely shared words may match the English source. Preserve message constraints such as character limits in every language. `tests/i18n.test.ts` checks all four dictionary categories, the seven-language order and locale-aware paths.

The Private Commissions form requires full name, email, project category, location, project stage and a brief description. Company/organisation and expected timeline are optional. No budget field is requested. Category labels correspond to Private Residence, Luxury Hospitality, Superyacht, Private Aviation and Professional Partnership; stages correspond to Concept, Planning, Development, Refit and Other. Translated option labels map to stable internal values, and the selected locale accompanies the request. Retreat and consultation requests require full name, email and a message, with preferred timing optional. All forms require acknowledgement of the privacy notice. Client and server errors, sending state, success state and email fallback use the chosen language. A successful message must follow the configured provider's response; translated success text is not evidence that email delivery is configured or tested.

`home.workWithUs` contains the localised title and two editorial blocks for private clients and professional partners. Their links use `?client=private#enquiry` or `?client=professional#enquiry`, retaining the current language path and other parameters. The professional link selects Professional Partnership; the private link leaves the category open because a private project may concern land, sea or air. The mounted form responds without discarding other entered text. Ordinary section navigation preserves the chosen category. Browser history and initial URL selection are supported, and language links retain the selected URL profile. Legacy client-profile dictionary keys remain for compatibility, but the public form no longer has a separate client-profile field.

## Commercial meaning to preserve

The opening section must make the creator-and-integrator role clear: BMYGYM conceives, designs, selects and coordinates complete environments, combining its own Signature Concepts with carefully selected premium products from specialist manufacturers. Private clients and professional partners are both addressed, including larger property and hospitality projects.

The Private Longevity Blueprint starts through a formal, paid commission. It is presented in three steps: understanding the individual, defining the environment and creating the Blueprint. Scope, deliverables and fees are agreed before design begins. Implementation requires a separate agreement. Later design, selection, manufacturing coordination and integration can be commissioned according to project needs. Do not imply free design work, publish prices, include invented fees or make every later phase part of the initial commission.

AERO ONE is a transformable recovery and longevity chair concept for private aviation, in development, neither certified nor commercially available. Aircraft-specific technical feasibility, safety and aeronautical certification requirements apply to its development and integration. Its new supplied image and its localised caption must not suggest an installed or certified product. Other Signature Concepts remain design visions in development.

The editorial blog, new videos, complete BMYGYM Environment visualisation, immersive or virtual tour and Private Longevity Circle belong to a later phase. No new image, tour, public membership offer or article publication is introduced by this localisation update. Editorial articles require the user's review before publication.

## Selector, typography and mobile behaviour

The header selector shows a compact uppercase language code, such as `EN` or `AR`, with a restrained chevron. Its disclosure lists all seven native language names and codes in the specified order, with the current language identified. Links switch to the corresponding page and preserve the query and anchor, including when opened in another tab and on Retreats or consultation pages. Escape closes the disclosure and restores focus; an outside pointer also closes it. Both selector labels and navigation labels are localised.

The desktop header navigation begins at 1440px to accommodate longer translated labels. Below that width, navigation uses the disclosure menu. At 600px or less, the separate header enquiry button is hidden; enquiry remains available from the mobile menu and hero. Content wraps without shrinking supporting text below the established readable size.

Bodoni Moda and Manrope continue to serve Latin text locally. Russian sans-serif text uses the local Manrope Cyrillic subset. Because Bodoni Moda lacks Cyrillic coverage, local Cormorant Garamond normal and italic faces provide the Cyrillic serif fallback. All supplemental fonts originate from official Google Fonts sources and retain their SIL Open Font License notices, including `public/fonts/Cormorant-Garamond-OFL.txt`. See `docs/asset-manifest.md` for files and sizes.

Arabic headings use local Noto Naskh Arabic and body/interface text uses local Noto Sans Arabic. Their fallback order precedes system fonts so the supplied Arabic faces are actually selected; Latin brand names retain the original faces. Arabic has natural letter spacing, appropriate line height and no synthetic italic styling. Logical CSS properties mirror alignment, borders and spacing with RTL; only directional arrows mirror. Images and the Latin logotype do not. Free-text form controls use `dir="auto"`, email controls use `dir="ltr"`, and `bdi` or balanced isolates preserve mixed content. The logotype's visible Latin text is marked separately from its localised accessible label.

## Analytics consent and welcome preferences

The consent notice is localised in all seven `*-common.json` dictionaries through `cookieNotice.title`, `body`, `policy`, `dismiss` and `accept`; `footer.cookieSettings` labels the reopening control. The two actions mean “Essential only” and “Allow analytics”. The policy link resolves to the current language's `/cookies` page. Google Analytics may load only when analytics are enabled for the environment and the visitor has accepted. Consent wording and cookie policy must accurately describe measured visits, commission/contact clicks and provider-confirmed enquiries; no names, email addresses or other form content are sent in enquiry events. Revocation stops subsequent measurement and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies. Advertising cookies are not used.

The consent preference uses `bmygym.cookie-consent.v2` in local storage for 180 days, shared across languages on the same origin. The earlier informational dismissal under `bmygym.cookie-notice.v1` is not analytics consent. If storage is blocked, the choice lasts only for the current page. A separate `bmygym.welcome.v1` preference remembers closure of the welcome introduction for 180 days. Language changes must retain these preferences rather than create per-language records. The URL still determines the language. The earlier [cookie-notice.md](cookie-notice.md) describes the 28 October informational notice; its no-analytics and old-storage statements are historical, not the current consent contract. See [validation.md](validation.md) for actual runtime checks.

The Arabic welcome introduction is fully translated, including title, service, disciplines and Land / Sea / Air labels. The six earlier locales retain their original English welcome identity wording. Welcome copy now lives in all seven home dictionaries, with an explicit identity language; BMYGYM remains Latin.

Keep dictionary structures aligned, verify the selected locale and all home, legal and booking-page routes, and inspect desktop and mobile wrapping in all seven languages. Exercise keyboard navigation, Escape behaviour, language switching, required-field errors and sending/error/success messaging. For consent copy, also check notice wrapping, the localised policy link, acceptance, refusal, footer reopening, revocation and choices shared across languages. Check Russian and Arabic font coverage, the fonts actually used for rendered text, and local font requests. Include Arabic RTL, mixed text, welcome, not-found page and text enlarged to 200%. Confirm that AIR qualifications, paid independent Blueprint terms and pending-confirmation booking language remain visible and accurate.
