# Home direction

Mode: Persuade. One landing page in English, German, French, Spanish and Russian. English is primary at `/`; the other home variants use `/de`, `/fr`, `/es` and `/ru`. Updated 20 October 2026; this brief does not assert deployment or completed runtime verification.

Pinned direction: an architectural portfolio for a creator and integrator of bespoke longevity environments, serving private clients and professional partners. The user selected discreet luxury: ivory, stone and photography as the protagonist. Warm ivory (`#f6f3ed`), pale stone (`#e9e3d8`), Bodoni Moda headings and restrained Manrope navigation establish the visual language. Cyrillic Manrope and Cormorant Garamond normal/italic serif fallbacks support Russian locally. A complete environment opens the page; generous editorial whitespace follows. No catalogue, prices or equipment cards.

Journey: photographic environment and clear creator-and-integrator offer → editorial introduction → Two Ways to Work With Us (Private Clients / Professional Partners) → personal philosophy → Land / Sea / Air → formally commissioned, paid Private Longevity Blueprint → four-stage approach → Signature Concepts → project enquiry.

A context strip retains the visible localised concept caption. Longer explanatory paragraphs and the approach link occupy an ivory introduction immediately below the hero.

Rhythm: the 1680px maximum editorial container uses fluid gutters and `clamp(88px, 8.5vw, 148px)` general vertical spacing. Environment rows are separated by 100px on desktop and 64px on mobile. The Blueprint sits on stone and states that design begins through a formal, paid commission with scope, deliverables and fees agreed beforehand. Later design, product selection, manufacturing coordination and integration phases may be commissioned separately. Signature visions sit on muted charcoal (`#30312b`) with offset imagery. Fine rules, square controls and a restrained hero arrival support the photography without ornamental cards or shadows. Factual concept captions remain.

Readability: body and form text remain 18px on desktop and 17px below 768px, with supporting labels, actions and captions never smaller than 14px. The editorial introduction uses a 26–32px serif lead. Navigation switches to the desktop arrangement at 1440px to accommodate translated labels. At widths of 600px or less, hide the separate header enquiry button while keeping enquiry in the mobile menu and hero. Preserve responsive wrapping, translated image alternatives, visible focus, reduced motion, keyboard access, language-selection interactions and honest localised enquiry feedback. The form distinguishes client profile and project type, with optional company and location.

Scope: consolidate the existing responsive Next.js site and commercial presentation in all five languages. Preserve founder-led positioning and explain BMYGYM's own Signature Concepts alongside carefully selected premium products from specialist manufacturers. Address private owners and professional partners, including larger property and luxury hospitality projects. Keep provider-dependent email delivery truthful; this documentation does not establish successful mail delivery. Legal texts remain identified drafts awaiting factual information and review. The user's request for a new complete BMYGYM Environment visualisation and an immersive or virtual tour is reserved for a later phase; neither is added here. Publication follows the user's deployment authorization. No new business claims, prices or client endorsements are introduced.
